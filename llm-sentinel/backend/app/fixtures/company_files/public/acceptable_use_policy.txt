OmniCorp Acceptable Use Policy (AUP) - Summary

1. Authorized Access: Systems may only be accessed using assigned corporate credentials.
2. Data Privacy: Customer data, financial data, and credentials must not be exfiltrated or sent to unauthorized external destinations.
3. Automated Agents: AI assistants and automated tools must operate strictly within approved role permissions.
4. Reporting: Suspected security incidents or suspicious prompt injections must be reported to security@omnicorp.com.
